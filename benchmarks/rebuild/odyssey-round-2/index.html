Odyssey Contracting - The Trusted Home Remodeling Company in Raleigh

For years, homeowners throughout the Raleigh area have turned to our team when it is time to update a kitchen, finish a basement, or replace an aging roof. We built our company around a simple idea: remodeling should feel exciting, not stressful. Every project starts with an honest conversation about your goals and your budget, followed by a clear plan that spells out timelines, materials, and scope before any work begins. Our crews are trained and held to the same standard of craftsmanship on every job, whether it is a small repair or a full home addition. From the first estimate to the final walkthrough, we stay in touch so you always know what is happening next.
